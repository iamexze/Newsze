**Subject:** Market data licensing enquiry — subscription analytics platform

Dear BSE Information Products team,

I am writing to request licensing options and indicative pricing for BSE market data.

**What we are.** Newze is a pre-launch subscription platform publishing Indian market news
and analytics over the web, to retail and professional subscribers. No brokerage, no order
routing, no execution. We hold no market data licence today.

**What we need from BSE specifically.** Sensex and BSE index levels, cash equity prices
across the listed universe, end-of-day files, corporate actions, bulk and block deals, and
surveillance list status. Where BSE derivatives data is available we would like that quoted
separately. The full requirement, including history depth, is in the attached specification.

**Points that will shape the quote:**

- We intend to **launch on end-of-day data** and add delayed and real-time tiers later.
  Please quote the three separately.
- A substantial part of our display is **derived values computed from licensed data** rather
  than the quotes themselves. Section 4 of the specification lists exactly what we compute,
  and we need to know how your licence treats it.
- We store history permanently for statistical analysis. **Question 16 asks what happens to
  stored data on termination** — a clear answer to that is important to us.

We are approaching NSE in parallel and will need both for complete coverage, so a proposal
that works alongside an NSE agreement is helpful. We would also welcome your view on whether
a direct agreement or an authorised redistributor suits a company at our stage.
