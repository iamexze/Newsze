**Subject:** Sublicensing enquiry — Indian market data for a subscription analytics platform

Dear `<vendor>` team,

We are scoping market data for a pre-launch subscription platform and would like to
understand what you can offer as an authorised redistributor.

**What we are.** Newze publishes Indian and global market news and analytics to retail and
professional subscribers over the web. Subscription business model. No brokerage, no order
routing, no execution capability. We hold no market data licence today and are running on
synthetic data while we scope cost.

**What we need.** Set out in full in the attached specification. In summary: NSE and BSE
index, cash and derivatives data including strike-level option open interest and
participant-wise positioning; end-of-day files; ban and surveillance lists; corporate
actions; **point-in-time index constituent history**; and roughly fifteen years of history.
Global coverage — US indices and Indian ADRs, FX including USD/INR, commodity benchmarks and
government bond yields — would be valuable from a single counterparty if you offer it.

**What would make your proposal competitive.**

1. **End-of-day first.** We intend to launch on end-of-day data. A strong end-of-day proposal
   is competitive with us even without a real-time offer. Please quote end-of-day,
   fifteen-minute delayed and real time as separate line items.

2. **Clarity on derived data.** Much of our display consists of values we compute — open
   interest build-up classification, put-call ratio, max pain, implied-volatility rank, index
   contribution attribution, historical statistics. Section 4 of the specification lists them.
   We need to know whether these are treated as licensed data under your sublicence.

3. **A clean answer on sublicensing authority.** Please confirm in writing which exchanges
   you are authorised to redistribute, for which uses, and what the underlying agreements
   permit us to do.

4. **Historical depth, priced separately.** Fifteen years of history matters more to us than
   millisecond latency. Please price the archive as its own line.

5. **Termination terms for stored data.** Question 16 in the specification. We store history
   permanently and need to know whether termination obliges us to delete it.

Our scale assumptions are in section 5. We would welcome an indicative quote against them,
plus a view on how cost scales as subscriber numbers grow.
