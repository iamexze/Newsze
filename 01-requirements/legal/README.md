# Legal Briefs — Phase 0 Gates

Three briefing packs to be sent to counsel in **week 1** of Phase 0. Counsel typically takes
2–3 weeks to respond, so these are the longest-lead items in the phase.

| Brief | Gate | Counsel type | Target turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| [brief-g1-market-data.md](brief-g1-market-data.md) | G1 | Securities / commercial (exchange licensing) | 3 weeks |
| [brief-g2-sebi-posture.md](brief-g2-sebi-posture.md) | G2 | **Securities regulatory (SEBI)** — the critical one | 3 weeks |
| [brief-g3-content-rights.md](brief-g3-content-rights.md) | G3 | Media / IP | 3 weeks |

**How to use these:** send as-is, with the linked product documents attached. Each brief
states the facts, shows exactly what the product does, and asks numbered questions. Ask
counsel to answer the numbered questions directly — a general memo on "fintech regulation"
is not what we need and will cost more while unblocking less.

**Responses go in:** `01-requirements/legal/responses/` (create on receipt), and each
conclusion gets a row in [`../../decisions.md`](../../decisions.md).

> ⚠️ These briefs were drafted by the product team. They describe our understanding of the
> product, not our understanding of the law. Where they characterise a legal position, treat
> that as the question being asked, not an assertion.
