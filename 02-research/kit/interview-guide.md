# Interview Guide — 45 minutes

**Rule above all others: do not pitch, describe or hint at the product before minute 25.**
Once a participant knows what you're building, everything they say afterwards is contaminated
by politeness. The first 25 minutes are the only clean data you will get.

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0 · Warm-up | 3 min | Comfort, consent, context |
| 1 · **The reconstruction** | 12 min | RQ1, RQ7 — what actually happened, concretely |
| 2 · The surprise | 7 min | RQ2, loop L2 |
| 3 · Trust | 5 min | RQ2 |
| 4 · Tools & money | 6 min | RQ5, RQ6 |
| 5 · Concept reaction | 10 min | RQ3, RQ4 |
| 6 · Close | 2 min | Referrals |

---

## Block 0 — Warm-up (3 min)
Consent (see [screener.md](screener.md)). Then:
> "Before we start — I'm not going to give you any market opinion, and I'm not selling
> anything. I'm just trying to understand how your day actually works. There are no right
> answers, and the most useful thing you can tell me is what's annoying."

1. How long have you been trading? What got you started?
2. What do you mostly trade now?

## Block 1 — The reconstruction (12 min) ⭐ *the most valuable block*

> "I want to walk through **this morning** — or the last trading day — in detail. Not what you
> usually do. What actually happened today."

3. **What time did you wake up? What was the first market-related thing you looked at?**
4. Then what? *(Keep going, step by step, until 9:15. Do not let them generalise. "Usually
   I…" → "What about today, specifically?")*
5. **How long did all that take, start to finish?** *(Note the number. RQ1 lives or dies here.)*
6. Which of those did you *have* to do, and which was just habit?
7. **What did you still not know at 9:15 that you wished you knew?** ⭐
8. When something global happened overnight — say the US market fell — how do you work out
   what that means for your positions here?
9. *(If they use GIFT Nifty)* What does the GIFT Nifty number tell you? What doesn't it tell you?
10. **Walk me through your phone — which apps did you actually open today?** *(Ask them to
    screen-share or read out the list. Stated tool usage and actual tool usage differ wildly.)*

> **Probes, used relentlessly:** "Why that one?" · "What were you looking for?" · "Did you
> find it?" · "What did you do with that information?" · "How long did that take?"

## Block 2 — The surprise (7 min)

11. **Tell me about the last time news caught you off guard while you had a position on.**
12. Where did you first hear it? How long after it happened?
13. What did you do in the next 10 minutes?
14. **What would have had to be true for you to have handled that better?**
15. How often does that happen — weekly? monthly?
16. Have you ever found out about something *after* the move had already happened? How did you feel?

## Block 3 — Trust (5 min)

17. **Who do you trust most for market information? How did they earn that?**
18. Has anyone you followed turned out to be wrong a lot? How did you find out?
19. **Does anyone you follow keep score of their own calls?** *(Expect: no. Note the reaction — surprise, cynicism, indifference. This is the Moat 2 temperature check.)*
20. If someone showed you their full track record — including the bad calls — would that make you trust them more, or less?
21. How do you feel about Telegram/YouTube tip channels? Have you used them? Do you still?

## Block 4 — Tools & money (6 min)

22. **What's the last market tool you paid for? What made you pay that day?** *(The trigger matters more than the tool.)*
23. Have you ever nearly cancelled something? What stopped you? What eventually made you cancel?
24. What's the most you'd pay per month for something that genuinely helped? What would it have to do?
25. **Which tool do you admire but never open?** ⭐ *(Exposes the gap between impressive and useful — directly steers P1 vs P2 feature priority.)*
26. If I took away all your tools except two, which two would you keep?

## Block 5 — Concept reaction (10 min) — *only now*

> "I'm going to show you two screens. They're rough. I want your honest reaction, including
> if it's useless."

**Show the Overnight Bridge prototype.**
27. **Without me explaining — what do you think this is telling you?** *(Silence. Let them read. Comprehension is the test.)*
28. What would you do with this?
29. *(Point at the waterfall)* What's this part saying?
30. **How many of those bars is too many?** *(RQ4 — we believe ≤6. Test it.)*
31. Is anything here you wouldn't trust? Why?

**Show the Impact Card — the three variants in randomised order.**
32. Which of these three is most useful? Why?
33. *(Point at "Not enough history to call this")* **What do you make of that?** ⭐
    *(RQ3. Does honesty read as credibility or as incompetence? This single answer decides
    a core design choice.)*
34. Does the "34 similar events" line change how much you believe the number?
35. **Would you share any of this in a group chat?** Which part?
36. What's missing?
37. If this existed and was free, would you open it tomorrow morning? What about in a month?

## Block 6 — Close (2 min)
38. Anything I should have asked but didn't?
39. Do you know 2–3 people who trade seriously that I could speak to?
40. Would you be willing to try an early version?
