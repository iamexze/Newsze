# Recruitment Screener

**Target:** 20 interviews (12 P0, 4 P1, 4 P2) + 8 diary participants
**Over-recruit:** 40 qualified responses to land 20 completed sessions
**Incentive:** ₹1,000 voucher per interview · ₹2,000 per completed diary study
**Channels:** trading communities and meetups · broker referrals · X/Twitter · Reddit
(r/IndianStreetBets, r/IndiaInvestments) · existing network. **Not** paid panels — they
produce professional respondents.

## Screener questions

**S1. In the last 3 months, how often have you placed a trade or investment order?**
☐ Daily · ☐ A few times a week · ☐ Weekly · ☐ Monthly · ☐ Rarely · ☐ Never
→ *Never/Rarely = **disqualify***

**S2. Which of these do you trade or invest in?** *(multi)*
☐ Indian stocks (delivery) · ☐ Indian F&O (Nifty/Bank Nifty/stock options) · ☐ US stocks ·
☐ Mutual funds / SIPs · ☐ Gold / commodities · ☐ Crypto · ☐ Bonds / FDs only
→ *Bonds/FDs only = **disqualify***

**S3. Roughly how much do you have deployed in markets?**
☐ <₹1L · ☐ ₹1–5L · ☐ ₹5–25L · ☐ ₹25L–1cr · ☐ >₹1cr · ☐ Prefer not to say
→ *Not a disqualifier; used for segmentation*

**S4. How long have you been trading or investing?**
☐ <1 yr · ☐ 1–3 yrs · ☐ 3–5 yrs · ☐ 5–10 yrs · ☐ 10+ yrs
→ *<1 yr = **disqualify** for P0 (we need established habits)*

**S5. In a typical week, how much time do you spend reading market news or analysis?**
☐ <1 hr · ☐ 1–3 hrs · ☐ 3–7 hrs · ☐ 7–15 hrs · ☐ 15+ hrs
→ *<1 hr = **disqualify***

**S6. Do you currently pay for any market tool, data service, newsletter or subscription?**
☐ Yes → *which?* `__________` · ☐ No, but I have before · ☐ Never
→ *Not a disqualifier, but **at least 10 of 20 must be current payers*** (they can speak to value)

**S7. On a trading day, do you look at anything before the market opens at 9:15?**
☐ Yes, regularly · ☐ Sometimes · ☐ Rarely · ☐ No
→ *Direct probe on **RQ1**. Recruit a mix — we need people who **don't** do this too, or we'll only hear from believers*

**S8. Which do you use at least weekly?** *(multi)*
☐ TradingView · ☐ Sensibull · ☐ StockEdge · ☐ Trendlyne · ☐ Screener.in · ☐ Moneycontrol ·
☐ Economic Times / Mint · ☐ Zerodha Pulse · ☐ Telegram channels · ☐ YouTube · ☐ X/Twitter ·
☐ Broker app only

**S9. Your role:**
☐ Individual trading my own money · ☐ Individual + I advise friends/family informally ·
☐ Professional: analyst / RIA / fund / family office · ☐ I work at a broker or fintech ·
☐ Financial content creator
→ *"Professional" → **P2 quota**. "Broker/fintech" → **P3 conversation**, separate track*

**S10. City / state:** `__________` → *target ≥6 of 20 from non-metro*

**S11. Age:** ☐ 18–24 · ☐ 25–34 · ☐ 35–44 · ☐ 45–54 · ☐ 55+ → *under 18 **disqualify***

**S12. Would you be willing to do a short twice-daily log for 2 weeks (₹2,000)?**
☐ Yes · ☐ No → *Yes pool = diary candidates*

**S13. Availability for a 45-minute video call in the next 3 weeks** `__________`

confirms itself and the research is worthless.*
