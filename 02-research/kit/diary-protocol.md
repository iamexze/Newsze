# Diary Study Protocol

**Why:** interviews systematically distort frequency and duration. People remember their best
morning, not their median one. **8 participants × 10 trading days × 2 entries/day** gives the
real distribution. This is how we actually answer RQ1 and RQ7.

| | |
|---|---|
| Participants | 8, drawn from S12 "yes" (6 × P0, 2 × P1) |
| Duration | 10 consecutive trading days (2 calendar weeks) |
| Entries | 2/day — 07:45 and 15:45 IST |
| Channel | **WhatsApp** — one message, one-tap replies. Not an app, not a form. Friction kills diary studies |
| Incentive | ₹2,000 on completion of ≥16 of 20 entries |
| Reminder | Automated at 07:45 and 15:45; one nudge at +30 min, then let it go |

---

## Morning entry (07:45 IST)

> **Good morning. 4 quick taps 🙏**
>
> **1. Have you checked anything market-related yet today?**
> `Yes` / `Not yet`
>
> **2. If yes — roughly how long so far?**
> `<5 min` / `5–15` / `15–30` / `30–60` / `60+`
>
> **3. What did you check?** *(tap all)*
> `Telegram` `YouTube` `Moneycontrol/ET` `TradingView` `Broker app` `X/Twitter`
> `GIFT Nifty` `US markets` `WhatsApp groups` `Nothing` `Other`
>
> **4. One line: what do you still not know that you wish you did?**
> *(free text — this is the most valuable field in the study)*

## Afternoon entry (15:45 IST)

> **Market's closed. 3 taps 🙏**
>
> **5. Did anything surprise you today?** `Yes` / `No`
> → *if Yes:* **What, and when did you find out about it?**
>
> **6. Was there a moment today you wished you'd known something earlier?**
> `Yes` / `No` → *if Yes:* **What?**
>
> **7. How would you describe today?**
> `Smooth` / `Reactive` / `Confused` / `Didn't trade`

## Friday extra (one per week)

> **8. This week, which source actually helped you most? Which was pure noise?**
> **9. Anything you paid for this week — or nearly cancelled?**

## What we extract

| Metric | From | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Median pre-open minutes** (and the distribution, not the average) | Q2 | **RQ1** |
| % of trading days with any pre-open routine | Q1 | RQ1 |
| Source count per morning; which sources recur | Q3 | RQ1, RQ6 |
| **The "didn't know" corpus** — 80 free-text lines | Q4 | ⭐ Product's reason to exist, and ready-made UI copy |
| Surprise frequency and discovery lag | Q5 | **Loop L2** sizing — how often does the Interrupt actually fire? |
| Information-regret frequency | Q6 | RQ1 severity |
| Day-shape distribution | Q7 | Context |

## Analysis rules
1. **Report the median and the spread, never the mean.** One 90-minute outlier would otherwise manufacture the result we want.
2. **Count days with zero routine.** If 4 of 10 mornings have no pre-open check, the L1 habit thesis is weaker than our personas assume — and we need to know that in Phase 0.
3. **Code the Q4 free text into themes.** Expect clusters like *"what will open at"*, *"why did US fall"*, *"what's happening with my stock"*, *"is this news already priced in"*. The largest cluster is the product's headline promise.
4. **Compare each diary participant's logged minutes against what they said in their interview.** The gap between the two is itself a finding, and it is the reason this study exists.

## Ethics
No account details, no P&L, no position data is ever requested. Participants may skip any
entry. Data is anonymised at intake and deleted after 12 months. Non-completers are paid
pro-rata, not nothing.
