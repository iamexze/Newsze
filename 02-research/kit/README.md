# Research Kit — Phase 0

Everything needed to run the Phase 0 research. Launch recruitment on **day one** — it takes a
week to fill and the interviews gate the week-4 synthesis.

| Instrument | Purpose | RQs |
|---|---|---|
| [screener.md](screener.md) | Recruit the right 20 people (and screen out the wrong ones) | — |
| [interview-guide.md](interview-guide.md) | 45-min semi-structured interview | RQ1, RQ2, RQ5, RQ6, RQ7 |
| [diary-protocol.md](diary-protocol.md) | 8 traders × 10 trading days, twice daily | RQ1, RQ7 |
| [concept-test.md](concept-test.md) | 150 respondents, 3 Impact Card variants | RQ3, RQ4 |
| [synthesis-template.md](synthesis-template.md) | Turn it all into a written verdict per RQ | all |

**Research questions** are defined in [`../research-plan.md`](../research-plan.md) §1.

## Rules
1. **Never pitch in the first 25 minutes.** Ask about last Tuesday morning, concretely.
2. **Past behaviour, not stated preference.** "Would you use this?" is worthless. "Show me what you opened this week" is evidence.
3. **Affinity-map after every 5 interviews** and adjust the guide. Patterns found at interview 6 should shape interviews 7–20.
4. **Write the verdict even when it's inconvenient.** RQ1 failing is the cheapest possible outcome of this phase.
