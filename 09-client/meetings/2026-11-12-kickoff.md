## 1. Brief as received
Build a product-grade website that gathers **global news**, **global trading analytics** and
**Indian trading analytics**. Requirements emphasised by the client:
- It must be a **product**, planned the way a senior product manager would plan it —
  everything costed and sequenced, not built ad hoc.
- **Plan first.** A model of how the thing works before any code.
- It should be **hard to replicate** — "no one should be able to copy this."
- It should be **deep**: many features, significant detail.
- Work proceeds in **phases**, with the client reviewing and updating between them.

## 2. What was produced in this session
The full planning repository — overview, requirements (PRD, product model, user stories,
roles, scope, compliance), research, design (sitemap, flows, wireframes, design system),
architecture (system design, tech stack, 10 ADRs), data model, API, planning (roadmap,
milestones, estimates, metrics, instrumentation), ops and client documents.

## 3. Key positions taken (for client confirmation)
| # | Position | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The moat is a compounding Event→Outcome corpus plus a public Accuracy Ledger**, not features | Features are copyable in weeks. A three-year scored track record is not. Directly answers "no one should replicate this" |
| 2 | **India-first, global-ready.** MVP = "the best pre-open 10 minutes in India" | A narrow, deep daily habit beats a broad shallow product. Architecture stays global from day one |
| 3 | **Analytics, never advice** | SEBI RA regulations. Enforced in the schema, the prompts and CI — not just a disclaimer |
| 4 | **Three legal gates (market-data licence, SEBI posture, content rights) are go/no-go before production code** | These are the three things most likely to kill the project, and they are cheapest to discover now |
| 5 | **The Accuracy Ledger runs in shadow mode from Phase 1**, public in Phase 4 | It needs 90+ days of history before it can be shown. Sequencing decided now or the moat slips 6 months |
| 6 | **Phase 0 is separately priced and cancellable** | Four of the biggest cost inputs are unknown until it closes |

## 5. Risks flagged to the client
1. 🔴 **Market-data licensing may be expensive or slow.** Mitigated by an EOD-first MVP.
2. 🔴 **SEBI may treat quantified impact estimates as research.** Could force registration or a reframing.
3. 🔴 **The Impact Engine may not work.** Spike S3 in Phase 0 tests this deliberately early. If it fails, we ship a strong news + analytics product and both compounding moats are lost — a smaller business, decided cheaply.
4. 🟠 Incumbents could copy the surface features — but not a scored history.
